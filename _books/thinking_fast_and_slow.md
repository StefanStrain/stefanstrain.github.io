This was easily one of the hardest and most interesting books I've ever read, purely because of the sheer density of information it contains. It took me several reads to feel like I was grasping all its contents, and it even forced me to develop a new system for reading: keeping a notebook by my side to jot down everything I found interesting or informational—basically, a book journal. It's a fascinating must-read for anyone trying to understand the two systems of the mind and why we sometimes make the choices we do.

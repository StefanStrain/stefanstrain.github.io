This is easily the book that first got me so interested in behavioral sciences, neuroscience, and the application of statistics and data science in biology. It's a revolutionary exploration of neuroplasticity, filled with remarkable stories of people whose lives have been transformed by their brain's ability to adapt and rewire itself. It's a great book that truly sparks curiosity about how the brain functions and shows the incredible potential of the human mind to heal and improve.
